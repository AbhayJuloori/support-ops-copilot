The Problem

Support teams had the data. They just couldn't act on it fast enough.

Every support queue was full of signals: angry wording, product names, repeated failures, refund threats, and urgent context. The problem was that those signals arrived faster than humans could triage them.

78%
of customers leave after one bad support experience.

When a ticket is misrouted, delayed, or summarized poorly, the customer does not experience an internal workflow problem. They experience silence.

⏱️

Wrong queue, wrong team

Manual routing wastes the first response window and sends urgent work through teams that cannot solve it.

🚨

SLA breaches nobody saw coming

Operations teams usually discover breach risk after the clock is already gone and escalation is expensive.

📊

Executives flying blind

Leaders need patterns, risk, and root causes. Raw ticket volume alone does not explain what is breaking.

The Solution

Four AI engines. One unified system.

The copilot turns unstructured customer messages into category, urgency, routing, and summary signals that support teams can act on immediately.

01

Ticket Classifier

Like a postal sorter that reads the letter before routing it — instantly, every time, at scale.

Reads the customer message and identifies what it is about: billing, technical support, shipping, account access, or product questions. Uses XGBoost + TF-IDF trained on 200K labeled tickets.

02

SLA Breach Predictor

A weather forecast for customer complaints — warns the team before the storm hits, not after.

Estimates breach probability before a ticket misses its deadline. XGBoost + SMOTE handles class imbalance, giving a 0–100% risk score per ticket.

LLM Ticket Summarizer

Turns a 500-word frustrated customer rant into three actionable lines — agents respond faster, with more context.
