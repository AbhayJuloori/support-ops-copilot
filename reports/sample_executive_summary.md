# Weekly Support Operations Summary

**Period:** Apr 28 – May 4, 2025

Support volume increased to **2,847 tickets**, up **12% versus the prior week**. The SLA breach rate rose to **18.3%**, and average resolution time reached **14.2 hours**, driven primarily by billing incidents, launch-related product questions, and seasonal demand pressure.

## Top 3 Root Causes

- **Billing system outage:** A payment gateway timeout on Apr 30 generated duplicate-charge concerns, refund requests, and account access tickets. Billing tickets accounted for the largest week-over-week increase and had the highest escalation density.
- **New product launch:** The May 1 release created a surge in setup, compatibility, and feature-discovery tickets. Most launch-related tickets were low complexity, but routing delays pushed several medium-priority cases close to SLA breach.
- **Holiday volume spike:** Reduced staffing coverage over the holiday period increased queue age across email and web channels. First-response delays were most visible in technical support and order-status workflows.

## SLA Performance

- **Billing:** 24.8% breach rate, 16.9h average resolution. Duplicate-charge and refund cases require faster triage and clearer escalation paths.
- **Technical:** 19.6% breach rate, 15.4h average resolution. Connectivity and configuration issues remain the largest source of high-priority backlog.
- **Shipping:** 13.1% breach rate, 11.7h average resolution. Carrier-delay inquiries increased, but most stayed within SLA after routing improvements.
- **General support:** 8.9% breach rate, 7.8h average resolution. Performance remains stable with low escalation risk.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Create a billing incident playbook** for duplicate-charge detection, refund eligibility, and proactive customer messaging.
2. **Add launch-week routing rules** that send setup and compatibility tickets directly to Tier 2 for the first 10 business days after release.
3. **Increase holiday queue coverage** with two overflow shifts focused on email intake, billing triage, and high-priority technical tickets.

Outlook: Ticket volume should normalize next week if the billing incident remains contained, but SLA risk will stay elevated unless launch-related technical questions are routed earlier in the workflow.
